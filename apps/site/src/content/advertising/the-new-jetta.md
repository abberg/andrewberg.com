The New Jetta was the Flash minisite for the launch of the fifth-generation Volkswagen Jetta in 2005, made by The Barbarian Group with Arnold Worldwide. It had sections for features, photos and video, souvenirs, and building a car of your own, and at its centre was explore, where the car itself was the navigation.

Explore put the Jetta on a stage that visitors could spin through a full turn, with a ring of bubbles floating around it, one for each story about the car.

As technical lead at The Barbarian Group, I built the explore section. The bubbles ran on a small 2.5D engine. Each one had a position in three dimensions, allowing those nearer the visitor to grow and spread out while those behind the car shrank and tucked in behind it. Each bubble eased toward its new position with a little overshoot, which gave the interaction a feeling of physicality.

The car itself was a sequence of photographs, one for each ten degrees of the turn. Neither the photographs nor the bubbles were truly 3D, but turning together they gave the feeling of a real, interactive 3D environment, at a time when Flash had no 3D of its own.
