# Untitled 003

Imported from the supplied untitled 003 folder, leaving the original untouched. Click or tap either robot to trigger its action; interaction also unlocks the bundled missile and explosion audio.

The soundtrack uses the public SoundCloud widget, loaded independently of scene startup and omitted from thumbnail mode. The first scene click/tap requests playback at 20% volume after a quarter-second delay, giving the missile launch effect a clear start; native controls are in the info panel. SoundCloud currently supplies a preview of the original track. If the player is blocked or unavailable, the scene and bundled effects continue to work.

Preserves legacy Three.js models, terrain, lighting, animation, and credits. Repairs empty raycasts, Retina/touch picking, invalid transition listener cleanup, and undefined startup delta. Thumbnail mode captures the final camera position without audio or animation timing dependencies.
