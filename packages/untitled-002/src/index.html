Info

Audio reactive geometry using song anlysis data from The Echo Nest API. The form is generated by combining a random walk algorithm and noise field values. The audio is an excerpt from Travis Stewart and Praveen Sharma's group Sepalcure. Heavy inspiration from Inigo Quilez's kindercrasher, and the works of Jeremiah Shaw & Danny Jones. File under: “Things I needed to get out of my system.”
