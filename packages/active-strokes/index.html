Active Strokes

A real-time take on Active Strokes: Coherent Line Stylization for Animated 3D Models (Bénard, Lu, Cole, Finkelstein and Thollot, NPAR 2012). Lines are found in each rendered frame, then tracked from frame to frame by active contours (snakes), so hand-drawn strokes can follow them coherently as the model moves.

Full Metal Panic Behemoth model and animations from Another Century's Episode R.

Runs on WebGPU. The settings panel is top right; H hides it, W swipes, Space pauses.
