Three ways of drawing sketchy outlines

Inverted hull

A copy of the model pushed out along its normals and drawn black from behind, so it shows as a rim. One ring per line, each nudged its own way. Cheap and steady, but only silhouettes and overlaps.

Screen-space edges

The model's normals, depth and parts are rendered to a buffer, and ink goes wherever neighbouring pixels disagree. That catches the creases inside panels too. Edges are extended along their own direction for overshoot, and the wander is seeded from the model's surface so the lines don't slide over it.

Contour strokes

Silhouette and crease edges are found on the mesh every frame, chained into curves and drawn as overlapping strokes that run past their ends, with what's hidden decided against a depth buffer.

Batara (Mobile Suit Crossbone Gundam) model and reaction animations from Another Century's Episode R. Walk cycle: "Mutant Walking" from Mixamo, retargeted with three.js's SkeletonUtils.
